![Concert poster: Kel Orind, live at the Splintered Drum, with the party at the barrier](/uploads/session_3/image3.png)

The party pulled security duty at the Splintered Drum for Kel Orind's show, and the night spiraled fast: a candy-themed gnome biker gang (the Loli-Pops) and a massive tree-biker gang (the Demon Trees) both showed up, along with a fortune-teller, a floating-book librarian, and a slime-trailing planar patron whose touch triggered borrowed memories.

Maze and Ricea kept a falling tree-biker and a thrown gnome from becoming stage disasters; Ricea then threw herself in front of a glowing silver sword to save the Demon Trees' leader, Wrong Tooth Justice, resisting a soul-pull from the blade and earning a serious favor. Jamrick stole a live bomb from a Loli-Pop rather than let it hit the crowd, taking the blast himself, then triggered his own pyrotechnic finale for Kel's set.

![A Loli-Pop, armed with the candy-cane shiv Jamrick would later steal](/uploads/session_3/image1.png)

The party held the room together — stage and bar intact, no Harmonium called — and earned 17 gold for the job. Pierre drew the attention of a fighter named Ogessa over drinks, and Harlan shared a quiet bar moment with a silent armored regular named Cinder.

![Pierre toasting Orc Fire at the bar](/uploads/session_3/image2.png)

Then Cinder's helmet came off during the final chaos, and Harlan saw Maren Sable's face looking back at him.
