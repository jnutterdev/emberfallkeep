Bloom of Clover — a tabaxi bard fleeing a mysterious hooded figure and a message reading "REMEMBER" — woke in the same pod-lab pipeline the party had escaped weeks earlier, and was guided into Sigil by Pock before being hired alongside a hard-eyed guard named Cinder to play the Splintered Drum. That thread collided with the party's own the night Kel Orind's crowd was still buzzing.

![PSA poster: "Wanted: your nap, not your life — ent branches are not nap zones," Wrong Tooth Justice and Jamrick](/uploads/session_4/image10.png)

Harlan finally confronted Cinder about Maren Sable's face; she threw a punch, said nothing, and put her helmet back on. At the bar, an infernal named Azaire Klavviine offered Maze a soul-binding contract — Jamrick, briefly brilliant from his swollen-brain mutation, caught the trap and talked her out of signing. Wrong Tooth Justice confirmed Ricea had completed the Demon Trees' first initiation rite and now owes an Offering worth roughly 500 gold.

Then Clover played the impossible chord from her mentor's letter. The instrument fought her. The sound was wrong. Every member of the party began to glow, and nobody knows why.

![Clover shredding on stage, rock-concert style](/uploads/session_4/image7.png)

Leaving the Drum near 3 a.m., the party was ambushed in a dark Sigil alley by a wolf-faced attacker with a mortar and a squad of masked, self-duplicating enemies who explode into red smoke when killed. The session ended mid-fight, with the party still working out how to stop them.
