The party met an Athar street pamphleteer on the way to Kel Orind's job. His pitch: the gods aren't gods, just powerful beings playing the part, and belief is what gives that performance its weight. Sigil, he said, is the City of Doors — doors that can lead to other worlds, if you can find the right one.

He tried to get a party member to sign a petition declaring the gods false. When the pen touched the page, the character felt something real pull or burn at their spiritual connection, and stopped. In Sigil, apparently, belief isn't just opinion.
