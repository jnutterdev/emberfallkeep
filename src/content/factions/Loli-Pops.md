The Loli-Pops crashed Kel Orind's show at the Splintered Drum alongside their tree-biker rivals, the Demon Trees. They came armed with candy-cane blades, licorice-wrapped shivs, and at least one live explosive, which Jamrick stole out of a member's hand and took the blast from himself rather than let it hit the crowd.

Jamrick also lifted a candy-cane shiv off one of them mid-brawl and kept it. By the end of the night, his general unsettling presence had creeped several of them out badly enough that they left — not exactly a rivalry resolved, but not nothing either.
