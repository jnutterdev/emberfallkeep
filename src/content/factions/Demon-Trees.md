The Demon Trees crashed Kel Orind's show alongside their rivals, the Loli-Pops. Ricea Rhonie made an early impression, asking Oakhier about joining — Oakhier pointed them toward Wrong Tooth Justice directly.

That impression became something real when Ricea took a dangerous silver-sword strike meant for Wrong Tooth Justice's hand, resisting a soul-pull from the blade and surviving. Wrong Tooth recognized it as the Sacrifice — the first stage of Demon Trees initiation. The second stage, the Offering, requires Ricea to bring the gang something worth roughly 500 gold. They can always be found at the Splintered Drum.
