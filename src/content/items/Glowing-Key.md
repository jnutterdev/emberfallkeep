Maze reached into the slick center of the pod structure and pulled this out. The moment it came free, the pod system shut down — warm fluid rapidly cooling, air recycling stopping entirely, nearly drowning Jamrick before he was pulled loose.

Brought to the chamber's central mechanism, it triggered a sterile voice announcing "Experiment failed. Terminate the subjects" — and combat with the pod-lab's attendants and hulking creatures followed immediately. What the key actually is, and what became of it after the escape, remains unknown.
