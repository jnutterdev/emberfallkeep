Carried by a dark-skinned half-elf warrior who mostly watched the room at the Splintered Drum until Wrong Tooth Justice's brawl-energy put him in danger — he drew the blade to defend himself, aimed squarely at Wrong Tooth's hand.

Ricea Rhonie threw themself into its path instead. The physical wound was survivable, but the sword also pulled at Ricea's essence — a soul-drain Ricea resisted and survived. The warrior himself remains largely unknown; his sword clearly threatens more than flesh.
