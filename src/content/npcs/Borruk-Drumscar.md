Borruk runs the Splintered Drum with one clear rule: drink, dance, fight within reason, and don't break the bar unless you can pay for it. He served the party bottom-shelf drinks as part of their security payment and made sure they understood his good liquor costs real money.

He priced out the bottles and tubing for Jamrick's improvised pyrotechnics at fifteen gold, and reacted seriously when the floating-book librarian showed up at his venue — a rare tell for someone usually unbothered by Hive weirdness.
