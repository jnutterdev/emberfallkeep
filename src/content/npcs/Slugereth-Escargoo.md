Slugereth wandered through the Splintered Drum during Kel's show, leaving a trail of glowing slime behind him. A Loli-Pop who slipped into it briefly saw someone else's family or homeland memory. Jamrick later deliberately collected some on an apron and experienced a borrowed battle memory: a gem-studded dragon, squid-faced enemies, and a silver-sworded warrior fighting through the chaos.

The residue dried out by the end of the session — not something that can be bottled and kept, at least not yet.
