Cinder works security at the Splintered Drum, where Borruk Drumscar treats her as reliable, known muscle. She doesn't talk much. During Kel Orind's show she shared a quiet moment at the bar with Harlan (in bear form) — pouring a drink through her own helmet rather than removing it.

At the end of that night, her helmet came off in the chaos. Harlan saw her face and recognized Maren Sable — his murdered partner from his old life — though stronger, scarred down one side, and clearly a different person in some way he hasn't worked out yet. She reacted with anger, threw a punch, said nothing, and put the helmet back on.

She was also brought in alongside Clover to play the Drum after Kel's set, guiding Clover through her arrival in Sigil. The connection between Cinder and Maren Sable remains the party's biggest open question.
