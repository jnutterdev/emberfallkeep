Dane appears in Harlan's opening memory, from his life as a homicide detective in Mordent, before the pod-lab. He claimed to have followed the robed cultists Harlan was hunting and tried to squeeze extra gold out of him for the tip — an amount Harlan never actually agreed to.

Harlan shifted into bear form to make his point. Terrified, Dane gave up the real lead: the cultists came from Juniper Street and went into the basement of a Church of Pelor. Harlan marked him with a warning claw and let him go. Whether Dane exists anywhere the party can reach him now is unknown.
