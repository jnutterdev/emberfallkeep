description: Pierre's trainer, who warned him to throw his last underground fight and paid for it when Pierre refused.

Mac told Pierre the match was fixed — throw it in the third round, on Bootsie's orders, or Mac and his family would be in danger. Pierre didn't obey; he studied his opponent, dropped him before the third round, and won big.

Bootsie's people used Mac as leverage anyway, injured and threatened, before Pierre swung a bag of gold through what turned out to be an illusion of Bootsie himself. What happened to Mac after that — and whether he exists anywhere the party can find him now — is unknown.
