Wrong Tooth Justice leads the Demon Trees, the tree-biker gang whose entrance at the Splintered Drum shook the venue harder than the music did. Caught up in the brawl-energy of the room, he threw one of his own companions toward the stage — and then found himself facing a glowing silver sword when a half-elf warrior was pulled into the chaos.

Ricea Rhonie threw themself into the blade's path to save his hand, resisting a soul-pull from the sword and surviving. Wrong Tooth recognized the act as the first stage of Demon Trees initiation — the Sacrifice. The second stage, the Offering, requires Ricea to bring the gang something worth roughly 500 gold. He said the Demon Trees can always be found at the Splintered Drum.
