Before Sigil, Clover was teaching at Orianna's bardic college when a letter arrived bearing her name: the address of the Splintered Drum, and musical notation that didn't behave like music. A hooded figure appeared in the back of Clover's classroom, spoke her name, and eventually seized her wrists with a single word — REMEMBER — right before the ceiling came down.

Whether the hooded figure was Orianna, connected to her, or something else entirely hasn't been confirmed. When Clover was finally forced to play the impossible chord at the Splintered Drum, the whole party started to glow. Nobody knows why yet, and Orianna herself has not appeared in person.
