---

The party was walking back to Pock's near 3 a.m. when mist formed in the alley ahead and this figure fired a mortar into the middle of the group. Smoke bombs followed, and masked, self-duplicating attackers poured out to surround the party with range, elevation, and visibility all working against them.

Killing the masked attackers individually didn't seem to be enough — new ones kept appearing or duplicating from the smoke. The wolf-faced figure with the cannon appears to be the key to actually ending the fight, but the party hadn't solved it by the time Session 4 ended. Identity and motive unknown.
