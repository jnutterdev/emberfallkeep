Doctor Mestaphus set up shop at Kel Orind's show, reading fortunes for coin. When Wrong Tooth Justice got swept up in the brawl-energy of the room, he hurled the doctor across the venue — Maze caught him before he became a flying casualty.

He sells fortunes, and maybe nonsense, but Sigil has a way of making nonsense expensive.
