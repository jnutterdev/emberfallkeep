Pock pulled the party through a hidden hatch and a hand-built portal arch of scrap metal, books, and old swords, escaping the sword-bearing fiend that had cornered them in the corpse-lab. He didn't explain much — just poured himself a drink and left the party to figure out that they'd landed in Sigil, the City of Doors.

He runs a small shop and tenement in the Hive: food, tools, odds and ends, and a room upstairs for three gold every two weeks. He isn't warm about it — he treats rescuing people as a business transaction with a bill attached — but he's reliable, and he pointed the party toward their first job with Kel Orind.

Also currently dealing with a tenant, Grindle Three-Times, who owes rent.
