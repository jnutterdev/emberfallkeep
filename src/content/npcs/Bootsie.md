Bootsie expected Pierre to throw his fight in the third round and take it out on Mac the Knife and Pierre himself when he didn't. When Pierre swung a bag of gold at his head to end the confrontation, the bag passed straight through — Bootsie's presence was an illusion. The hammer that ended Pierre's old life fell moments later. Whoever or whatever Bootsie actually is remains unresolved.
