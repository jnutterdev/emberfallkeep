Grith appeared to Maze on a rooftop as she fled a forced confrontation with her old infernal ties. He told her that he'd led the others to her, and that more would be involved. Small gates opened; winged devils stepped through, and one seized her — Maze's last clear memory before waking in the pod-lab.

Given Azaire Klavviine's more recent approach with a soul-binding contract, Grith's reach may not be finished with her.
