Kel Orind hired the party as security for his show at the Splintered Drum — keep the crowd off the stage, keep the bar intact, keep the Harmonium from showing up. Six gold total plus drinks, not the good drinks.

He fronted Borruk's price for Jamrick's improvised pyrotechnics because a good explosion, in his professional opinion, would make the show unforgettable. He was right — the finale made his set genuinely memorable, for exactly the right and wrong reasons.
