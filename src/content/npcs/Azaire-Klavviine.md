Azaire Klavviine cornered Maze at the Splintered Drum, asking whether she'd been to Minauros and whether she had ties to the Nine Hells, invoking Dispater by name. He offered her a contract, framed as something that could give her whatever she desired.

Jamrick — briefly brilliant from his swollen-brain mutation, and inspecting the document while dressed in lingerie and an apron — read the fine print and realized it would bind Maze's soul to Azaire's master at the moment of her death. He warned her not to sign. Maze asked if a fairer version could be written; Azaire didn't pursue it, pocketed the contract, and left. The offer is presumed still open.
