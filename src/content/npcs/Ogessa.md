Ogessa entered the Splintered Drum with her companion Aggriel "Pennyfang" Copperskein and drew attention immediately — Borruk recognized her on sight. She pulled Pierre to the bar, bought him Orc Fire, and watched with open delight to see if he could handle it (he could, mostly).

By the end of the night she'd called him "pretty boy," grabbed him, and kissed him hard enough that Pierre still thinks about it. She may be able to get him set up with a fight — once his hand is fixed.
